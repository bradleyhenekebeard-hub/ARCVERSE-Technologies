# Company GNOME Flagship Homepage Design

## Intent

Make Company GNOME the primary ARCVERSE technology story so visitors immediately understand that ARCVERSE offers more than individual workflows: it offers a private, governed agentic operating intelligence layer that can connect approved company knowledge, evidence and operational information.

## Goals

- Lead the homepage with Company GNOME as the flagship technology.
- Use the exact technical description: **private, governed agentic operating intelligence layer**.
- Retain **The heartbeat behind smarter business operations.** as the supporting emotional line and preserve the existing heartbeat artwork.
- Explain GNOME in plain language and show a clear flow from scattered inputs to durable company memory, traceable evidence, management attention and human-controlled action.
- Reframe existing tender, finance, logistics, telematics, contract, audit and automation content as examples of what GNOME can power.
- Keep the page premium, responsive, accessible, performant and search-friendly.

## Non-goals

- Do not represent GNOME as a generic chatbot or autonomous decision-maker.
- Do not claim completed customer deployments, production readiness, certifications or integrations that are not evidenced.
- Do not redesign the logo, remove the ARCVERSE welcome animation, alter the slogan **Powered by AI. Guided by Faith.**, or weaken VELA's development/readiness wording.
- Do not deploy or merge without the user's explicit approval after preview verification.

## Chosen Approach

Use a GNOME-first flagship homepage. The hero becomes the primary product introduction; a dedicated GNOME intelligence section immediately below demonstrates the operating model; the existing problem and solution content remains but becomes evidence of the broader GNOME platform story. This was selected over a copy-only hero change, which would not explain the differentiation, and a separate GNOME page, which would make the flagship less immediate.

## Content and Hierarchy

1. Navigation leads with **GNOME** and links to `#gnome`.
2. Hero eyebrow: **ARCVERSE FLAGSHIP TECHNOLOGY**.
3. Hero title: **Company GNOME** followed by **The agentic intelligence layer for your entire business.**
4. Supporting line: **The heartbeat behind smarter business operations.**
5. Hero explanation describes approved company knowledge, evidence, workflows and operational information becoming governed, traceable intelligence.
6. Primary action explores GNOME; secondary action starts a business discussion.
7. The GNOME section visualises: **Company inputs -> governed company memory -> management intelligence -> human-controlled action**.
8. The section explains four differentiators: company context, evidence traceability, management attention and controlled expansion.
9. Existing use-case sections are introduced as capabilities GNOME can power, not as unrelated products.

## Visual Direction

Preserve the established deep navy/black ARCVERSE canvas, cyan signal colour and restrained champagne gold. Reuse the heartbeat/ECG motif as GNOME's living intelligence signal. Replace generic orbit labels with GNOME-relevant concepts such as **KNOWLEDGE**, **EVIDENCE** and **ACTION**. Keep the composition centred, deliberate and compact without reintroducing empty space or a large right-side card.

## Accuracy and Readiness

GNOME may be described as ARCVERSE's flagship technology and a private, governed agentic operating intelligence layer. Public copy may describe the intended operating model and scoped applications. It must state that integrations and deployment scope are confirmed for each organisation, and it must not imply unsupervised actions or universal live availability.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The first visible product name and primary heading are GNOME-first.
- The exact technical term and heartbeat line are visible on the homepage.
- A dedicated `#gnome` section explains the operating intelligence flow and human control.
- Existing use cases remain discoverable and are explicitly connected to GNOME.
- Existing brand, contact, animation, VELA, accessibility and reduced-motion safeguards remain intact.
- Automated tests, desktop review and 390x844 mobile review pass with no horizontal overflow or browser warnings/errors.

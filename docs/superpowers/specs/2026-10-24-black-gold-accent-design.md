# Black and Gold Accent Design

## Intent

Refine the approved ARCVERSE redesign so it feels more distinctive and premium while preserving its technology identity, readability and conversion clarity.

## Chosen direction

Use a hybrid palette: retain the black/deep-navy foundation and electric-blue ARCVERSE logo, replace most violet decorative accents with restrained champagne gold, and keep cyan for operational signals and interactive clarity.

## Considered approaches

- Keep the current blue/cyan/violet palette: strongly technical, but less personal to the founder's preference.
- Convert the full interface to black and gold: luxurious, but risks clashing with the blue logo and looking ornamental rather than enterprise-technology focused.
- Use black/navy with champagne-gold accents and retained blue/cyan signals: selected because it adds authority without losing the existing brand or accessibility.

## Exact visual rules

- Foundation remains near-black and deep navy.
- Gold is a muted champagne tone, used for section labels, fine borders, selected nodes and restrained emphasis.
- Violet is removed from primary gradients and decorative nodes.
- Cyan/blue remains on the logo, system signals, focus indication and operational storytelling.
- Primary calls to action remain light with dark text for strong contrast.
- No large metallic gradients, glossy gold buttons or luxury-jewellery styling.
- Preserve the welcome animation, centred hero, existing content, VELA readiness language and responsive structure.

## Success criteria

- The palette reads as premium enterprise technology, not fashion or luxury retail.
- Text and controls retain clear contrast.
- Desktop and mobile compositions remain unchanged and free of overflow.
- Reduced-motion and focus-visible behaviour remain intact.

Personal finance features

Less paperwork. More financial clarity.

The system is designed to make household administration easier to keep up with — not to create another job for you.

Receipts, invoices & bills

Photograph or upload documents and let ARCVERSE capture the important details, including supplier, date, amount and category.

Bank statement cross-checking

Upload your bank statement and compare transactions against your receipts and recorded spending so missing or unmatched items stand out.

Income & expenditure

Track money coming in and going out across the household, with a clearer monthly view of where it is being spent.

Monthly bills

Keep recurring costs such as rent or bond, car payments, insurance, medical aid, subscriptions and utilities in one place.

Budget & savings

Build a realistic budget from actual spending, compare the plan with reality and track savings goals over time.

Family receipt submissions

Add family members with restricted permissions so they can submit their own receipts without automatically seeing the household’s wider bills or private financial information.

Unaccounted transactions

When the bank statement shows spending that has no matching record, ARCVERSE can call it out so you can categorise or explain it.

Search & records

Keep receipts and supporting documents organised by date and category instead of searching through messages, drawers or folders.

Export your figures

Export income and expenditure data to Excel when you want a spreadsheet, a report or a clean record outside the app.

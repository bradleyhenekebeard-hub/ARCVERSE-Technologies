Household money management · South Africa

Looking for a budgeting or expense tracking app?

Whether you call it a family budget planner, personal finance app, household expense tracker or digital receipt organiser, the goal is the same: understand your household income, bills and spending without losing the supporting paperwork.

How can I track household expenses?

Start with your income and recurring bills, then record day-to-day spending and keep receipts where possible. A monthly expense tracker helps you review groceries, transport, school costs, subscriptions and other spending categories together.

How do I make a family budget?

List household income, fixed expenses and variable spending. Compare your planned amounts with actual records during the month, then adjust the next month's budget. KAYA is designed around this household budgeting process.

Can I keep receipts and bills in one place?

The KAYA workflow brings documents and expenditure records together. Explore receipt uploads, household bills and document organisation in the app; automated receipt reading may depend on the version and enabled features.

Can it check my bank statement?

Bank-statement import and transaction cross-checking are shown as an intended workflow. Check the current app version and supported statement formats before depending on automatic reconciliation.

Is it suitable for a shared household?

KAYA is designed around separate household accounts and family access. Confirm current permissions before inviting someone to view or submit financial information. It is a record-keeping tool, not a bank account.

Does the free trial or payment start now?

No. The website describes proposed Personal and Family plans, but online subscription payments, automatic trial activation and access restrictions are not currently active. Read the pricing information →

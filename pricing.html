How the commercial arrangement works

Upfront project payment

Discovery, configuration, development, integration and testing are priced according to an agreed written scope and milestones.

Monthly service where needed

Hosting, maintenance, support or managed automation are listed separately. Any AI/API allowances, caps and overages require written agreement.

Third-party dependencies

Accounting, telematics, communication and other vendors may require licences, API permissions or external fees. These are identified in the quote.

Final features, delivery dates, privacy, security, ownership, VAT, billing and support obligations are confirmed in each agreement. No savings or integrations are guaranteed without assessment.

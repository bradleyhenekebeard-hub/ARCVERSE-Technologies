Website design in South Africa · common questions

Looking for someone to build your business website?

ARCVERSE designs websites for businesses that want a clear online presence, whether you're opening a new company or replacing an outdated site. Here's what to consider before requesting a quotation.

How much does a small business website cost?

Our Digital Starter package begins at R2,999 once-off for a one-page business website. Multi-page websites and branding packages are available; the final scope, any applicable VAT and third-party costs are confirmed in a quotation. Compare website packages →

I need a website. What should I prepare?

Tell us what your business does, your services, preferred colours and contact details. If you already have a logo, domain or company profile, we can discuss how to use them in the design. Request a quotation for any content writing or additional work outside your chosen package.

Can you design a website and matching letterhead?

Yes. Business Identity and Premium Brand packages include a matching editable Word letterhead. We coordinate the website and business stationery so the same brand appears on screen and on your correspondence.

What kind of website do you need?

We can discuss a one-page company website, a multi-page website explaining your services, or a coordinated website and branding project. Responsive design, clear customer contact options and an agreed scope are part of selecting the right package. ARCVERSE operates online and works with customers across South Africa; domain, hosting, email and maintenance charges are quoted separately unless expressly included.

Already have a website? Tell us what you'd like to improve. We'll assess the requested redesign, additional pages or content and provide a quotation rather than promising a result before reviewing your site.

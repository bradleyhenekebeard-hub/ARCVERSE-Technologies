Full platform overview

What ARCVERSE actually does.

Everything below is integrated into one business-aware workspace. Ask it in plain language, talk to it by voice, or let it organise the background admin so you can stay focused on decisions.

01 / AI ASSISTANT

Your own AI assistant, trained on your business

Ask “Who owes us money?”, “What did we pay that supplier last time?”, or “Is this contract safe to sign?” ARCVERSE remembers your clients, prices, contracts, rules and preferences, drafts in your company’s voice, opens the right section on command, and prepares one-click briefings before calls or meetings.

  • Plain-language questions and voice dictation
  • Draft letters, clauses, emails and briefings
  • Remembers your standards and preferences
Owner-confirmed working feature
02 / EMAIL

Your inbox, finally connected to your business

ARCVERSE reads your business mailbox, works out which client, supplier or job an email belongs to, and files the conversation with the work itself. It suggests the next step, drafts the reply, attaches quotes, invoices or contracts, and sends from your own email address only when you approve it.

  • Files emails against clients, suppliers or jobs
  • Drafts replies and attaches the right documents
  • Scam-resistant approach: email text alone is not treated as instruction
Customer-specific mailbox setup
03 / MONEY

See where money comes from, where it goes, and where it leaks

Photograph receipts, invoices or bills and ARCVERSE reads the supplier, date, amount, VAT and category. At month end it shows what came in and went out, who owes you, who you owe, what is due next month, what can be claimed, your VAT position and what should be set aside for SARS. It also builds the month-end spreadsheet and email pack for your bookkeeper.

  • Receipt capture, debtors, creditors and month-end packs
  • Profit per job, quotation comparison and overdue chasing
  • Budgets, break-even, loans, commissions and exchange-rate support
Working feature with financial review process
04 / LOGISTICS

Every load, truck and delivery accounted for

Schedule collections and deliveries, load a whole fleet from one spreadsheet, send truck packs to the loading point, photograph weighbridge slips or delivery notes, and reconcile what was loaded against what was delivered. ARCVERSE tracks transporters, rates, drivers, vehicles and shortfalls on the day they happen.

  • Delivery schedules and transporter comparisons
  • Weighbridge and delivery note extraction
  • Fleet records, obligations and map-pin access
Owner-confirmed working feature
05 / DOCUMENTS

Professional paperwork in seconds, never the wrong figure

Create quotes, offers, LOIs, agreements, NDAs, invoices, letters, minutes, reports and more on your own letterhead. Describe the document in plain words and ARCVERSE fills it in from your data. If information is missing, it leaves the field blank rather than guessing. One source of truth means the quote, agreement and invoice stay aligned.

  • Word and PDF output on company branding
  • One-tick protection with NDAs and NCNDAs
  • Version-safe document edits that never overwrite originals
Human approval before external use
06 / CONTRACTS

Never sign something you didn’t agree to

Review contracts against your standard, show missing protections, compare returned versions and expose exactly what changed: deleted penalties, hidden discounts, moved jurisdictions or altered obligations. ARCVERSE also prepares factual update briefs for your lawyer in one click.

  • Compares returned contracts to your standard
  • Flags changed clauses and missing protections
  • Creates legal status briefs fast
Decision support tool, not a law firm

Clients, suppliers, leads and follow-ups

Keep every client and supplier in one place with contacts, signed documents, last price, history and due diligence. ARCVERSE tracks leads, turns prospects into live jobs, drafts follow-ups when a client goes quiet, and lets one search box find any document, note, PDF, invoice or email.

  • Lead tracking and live-job conversion
  • Follow-up prompts and reminders
  • Due diligence and company file management
Owner-confirmed working feature

People, teams and payroll

Generate payslips, handle PAYE, UIF, allowances, commissions and medical aid, and keep complete employee files together. ARCVERSE records staff contracts, bank confirmations, induction documents, employment offers, terminations and KPI reporting for teams or agents.

  • Payslips and payroll reports
  • Complete employee files with missing items flagged
  • Team KPI and activity reporting
Customer-specific payroll setup

Several companies, one system

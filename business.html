What could we solve in your business?

The following are practical solution examples available for scoping, not promises of already deployed functionality.

01 / SALES & PROCUREMENT

RFQs piling up?

THE PROBLEM

Staff lose time retrieving price lists and specifications. Responses are delayed or inconsistent.

THE PROPOSED FIX

Scope an intake and draft-response workflow using approved records, clear exceptions and human approval.

WHAT TO MEASUREResponse time, rework and missed deadlines

Delivery depends on: Access to authorised RFQs, approved data and decision-makers.

02 / DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT

Too much manual paperwork?

THE PROBLEM

Employees retype invoice, receipt and form information. Missing details create downstream errors.

THE PROPOSED FIX

Capture and validate structured information, flag missing fields and direct exceptions for review.

WHAT TO MEASUREProcessing time, missing data and correction rates

Delivery depends on: Representative documents, secure storage and verified extraction.

03 / TRANSPORT & LOGISTICS

Deliveries difficult to reconcile?

THE PROBLEM

Trips, proof of delivery and billing evidence live in separate systems and folders.

THE PROPOSED FIX

Connect authorised delivery records and check completeness before invoicing.

WHAT TO MEASUREMissing documents, unbilled deliveries and disputes

Delivery depends on: Reliable source data and any third-party access or APIs.

04 / FINANCE & CONTROL

Costs and lost revenue discovered late?

THE PROBLEM

Finance and operational teams rely on slow manual comparisons and follow-ups.

THE PROPOSED FIX

Create defined exception checks, review queues and escalation alerts.

WHAT TO MEASUREIssue detection and resolution times

Delivery depends on: Authorised finance records, rules and permissions.

05 / MANAGEMENT

Too little visibility into daily operations?

THE PROBLEM

Managers wait for updates and disagree about which numbers are correct.

THE PROPOSED FIX

Build role-based dashboards from agreed and validated sources, including data freshness indicators.

WHAT TO MEASUREReporting time, accuracy and follow-up activity

Delivery depends on: Source system access, definitions and governance.
